# Shree Gurudev Lights & Sound — Event Planning

Official website for **Shree Gurudev Lights & Sound**, Brahmapuri (Maharashtra).  
Specializing in grand weddings, birthday celebrations, school events, cultural programs, corporate events, religious gatherings, and premium tent & pandal setups since 1984.

---

## 🌟 Features

- **Modern & Responsive Design**: Elegant gold and dark luxury aesthetic with smooth animations.
- **Dedicated Event Pages**:
  - [Wedding Celebrations](wedding.html)
  - [Birthday Bashes](birthday.html)
  - [School Events](school.html)
  - [Cultural Programs](cultural.html)
  - [Corporate Events](corporate.html)
  - [Religious Gatherings](religious.html)
  - [Tents & Pandals](tent-pandal.html)
- **Interactive Galleries & Video Reels**: Showcase of past event photography and video setups.
- **Direct Inquiry & Booking**: One-tap WhatsApp chat integration, direct calling, and interactive contact form.
- **Google Maps Integration**: Interactive location map for the venue / office in Brahmapuri.
